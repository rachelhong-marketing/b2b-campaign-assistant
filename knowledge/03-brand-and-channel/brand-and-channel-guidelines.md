# Brand and channel guidelines (Fernhill Software)

> Made-up example. Replace with your own brand guidelines.

## Voice
- Plain, confident, practical. Talk like a smart colleague, not a billboard.
- Lead with the reader's problem, then the outcome.
- One idea per ad. One call to action.
- No hype words (see `rules/brand-words.csv`).

## Terminology
| Use | Don't use |
|---|---|
| Data Quality Platform | data cleansing tool, DQ tool |
| Data Readiness Assessment | quiz, survey |
| customers | users (in external copy) |
| AI-ready data | AI-powered data |

## Approved proof points
| Proof point | Source | OK to use until |
|---|---|---|
| Customers cut data cleanup time by 40% | 2025 customer survey, n=120 | 2026-12-31 |
| Teams spend 30% of their week fixing data | 2025 customer survey, n=120 | 2026-12-31 |
| Setup in under two weeks | Implementation records, 2025 median | 2026-12-31 |

Any number not on this list needs a source and approval before it runs.

## Channel best practices
**LinkedIn**
- Intro text: keep the point in the first 150 characters (the rest is cut off on desktop).
- Headline: about 70 characters or fewer.
- Show the asset (assessment screen, guide cover) in the image. Faces of stock models underperform for us.
- Test one variable at a time.

**Google Search**
- Put the problem or the offer in headline 1. Pin only when legal requires it.
- Use at least 8 distinct headlines so the system has options.
- Don't bid on competitor brand names without approval.

**Email**
- Subject: 50 characters or fewer, specific, no ALL CAPS.
- One CTA button. Repeat it once as a text link.
- Measure clicks and form fills, not opens.
