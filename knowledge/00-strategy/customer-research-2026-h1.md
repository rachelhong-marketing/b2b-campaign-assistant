# Customer research summary, 2026 H1

> Made-up example. Product marketing's summary of win/loss interviews, customer calls and a competitor message review. Internal only: these counts are not proof points for external copy.

## Sources
- 18 win/loss interviews (January to June 2026)
- 12 customer calls with data and analytics leaders
- Review of competitor home pages and ads (May 2026)

## What buyers told us
| Finding | How often |
|---|---|
| They treat duplicate records as a one-time cleanup project, then the duplicates come back within months | 11 of 18 interviews |
| Analytics leaders blame the reporting tool when reports don't match; the cause is usually duplicate customer records | 8 of 12 calls |
| The first question in every evaluation: "How do we know which record is the right one?" | Most calls |
| They worry merging records will break month-end reports | 7 of 18 interviews |
| Deals we lost went to "we'll clean it up ourselves this quarter" more often than to a competitor | 6 of 10 losses |

## Buyer questions to answer
- How many duplicates do we actually have?
- How do we decide which record is right?
- Will merging break our reports?
- Who owns this after the cleanup?

## What competitors say
| Competitor | What they own in the market |
|---|---|
| Clearline Data | "Fast cleanup": one-time cleanup projects |
| Datavault Pro | Governance and compliance |
| Purestream | Real-time data pipelines |

**White space:** nobody owns "keeping customer data clean after the cleanup." That's our monitoring story and matches the brand's 2026 direction.
