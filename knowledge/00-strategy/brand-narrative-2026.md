# Brand narrative, 2026 (Fernhill Software)

> Made-up example. This is the brand marketing input to campaign planning: where the brand is going this year, not product detail.

## Mission
Customer data people can trust.

## Brand pillars
1. **Trust:** numbers that agree, wherever you look.
2. **Simplicity:** plain language, fast setup, no big consulting project.
3. **Proof:** show the result, not the promise.

## Narrative direction for 2026
Move from "a tool you buy for a cleanup project" to "the way your team keeps customer data clean." Every campaign should make ongoing data quality feel normal and doable, not a one-off fire drill.

## Perception we want to shift
| Today buyers think | We want them to think |
|---|---|
| Data quality is a one-time cleanup project | Data quality is a habit, and the platform keeps it going |
| Data quality tools are for engineers | Analytics and data leaders own the outcome together |

## Tone constraints
Plain, confident, practical. No fear-based messaging and no hype words (see the brand guidelines).
