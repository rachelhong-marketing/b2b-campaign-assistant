# Target accounts and personas (Fernhill Software)

> Made-up example.

## Target account criteria (Data Quality Platform)
- **Industries:** Retail, Financial Services, Manufacturing
- **Company size:** 1,000 to 10,000 employees
- **Regions:** United States, Canada
- **Intent topics:** data quality, master data management, data governance, AI readiness
- **Exclude:** current customers, open opportunities in late stage, competitors (see `competitors.csv`)

## Personas

| Persona | Typical titles | What they care about | What they're measured on |
|---|---|---|---|
| Data leader | VP of Data, Head of Data Engineering | Fewer broken pipelines, less firefighting | Data uptime, project delivery |
| Analytics leader | Director of Analytics, BI Director | Trusting the numbers in reports | Report accuracy, time to insight |
| Finance leader | CFO, VP Finance | Cost of bad data, audit risk | Cost savings, risk |

Note: the CMO is not a target persona for the Data Quality Platform. Marketing leaders are a secondary audience only for the Customer Data add-on.
