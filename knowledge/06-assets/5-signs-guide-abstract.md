## What it covers
1. The same customer shows up as several records.
2. Key fields are often blank.
3. Each system defines "customer" differently.
4. Nobody owns data quality.
5. Fixes are one-time cleanups, so problems come back.

Each sign has a quick self-check and one first step.

## Proof points used in the guide
- Teams spend 30% of their week fixing data (2025 customer survey, n=120)
- Customers cut data cleanup time by 40% (2025 customer survey, n=120)
