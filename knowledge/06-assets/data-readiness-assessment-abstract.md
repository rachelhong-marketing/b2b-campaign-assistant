# Asset abstract: Data Readiness Assessment

> Made-up example. An asset abstract is the short description product marketing writes for each offer. The campaign assistant drafts landing pages and ad and email copy from it.

- **Asset:** Data Readiness Assessment
- **Type:** Online assessment, gated (work email required to see the full report)
- **Link:** https://yoursite.example/assessment
- **Time to complete:** 3 minutes, 15 questions
- **Cost:** Free
- **Funnel stage:** Interest
- **Best for:** Data leaders and analytics leaders planning an AI project in the next 12 months

## What it is
A short online assessment that scores how ready a company's customer data is for AI, from 0 to 100.

## What the taker gets
- A readiness score from 0 to 100
- A score for each of four areas: completeness, duplicates, consistency and governance
- The top three fixes to make first, ranked by impact
- A report they can download and share with their team

## The problem it answers
Teams start AI projects without knowing whether their data can support them. They find out when the project stalls.

## What happens next
- Score under 50: offer a demo with a data specialist to walk through the fixes.
- Score 50 or above: send the guide "5 signs your data isn't ready for AI" and the nurture.

## Proof points to use
Only the approved proof points in the brand guidelines. The assessment does not benchmark against other companies yet, so don't say it does.
