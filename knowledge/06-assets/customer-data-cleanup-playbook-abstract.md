# Asset abstract: Customer Data Cleanup Playbook

> Made-up example. An asset abstract is the short description product marketing writes for each offer. The campaign assistant drafts landing pages and ad and email copy from it.

- **Asset:** Customer Data Cleanup Playbook
- **Type:** Guide, 20 pages, PDF, gated (work email required)
- **Link:** https://yoursite.example/cleanup-playbook
- **Cost:** Free
- **Funnel stage:** Interest
- **Best for:** Data leaders and analytics leaders whose reports don't match because of duplicate customer records

## What it is
A 30-day plan to find, merge and stop duplicate customer records.

## What the reader gets
- A way to measure how many duplicate records you have
- How to set match rules for "same customer" across systems
- A safe order to merge records without breaking reports
- How to assign an owner so duplicates don't come back
- A one-page checklist to share with the team

## The problem it answers
The same customer shows up as several records, so reports don't match and teams spend their week fixing data by hand.

## What happens next
- Downloads from target accounts go to sales development for a demo offer.
- Everyone else goes to the nurture, which adds the webinar on 2026-08-03.

## Proof points to use
Only the approved proof points in the brand guidelines. The playbook isn't based on a count of customer projects, so don't say it is.
