# Paid social brief: 2026-Q1 Cost Control (APPROVED example)

> Made-up example of an approved brief, used to show the level of detail and tone. Don't copy its content into a new campaign.

## 1. Campaign link
- Master brief: 2026-q1-cost-control-master-brief
- Platform(s): LinkedIn
- Funnel stage(s): Awareness, Interest
- Launch and end dates: 2026-01-12 to 2026-03-27

## 2. Objective and KPIs
- Objective: Get finance and data leaders at target accounts to download the "Hidden cost of bad data" calculator.
- Primary KPI and target: 120 MQLs from LinkedIn
- Early-read metric (direction only): Click-through rate above 0.6%

## 3. Audience and targeting
- Targeting type: Account list (Q1 target accounts) plus job function
- Industries and company size: Retail, Manufacturing; 1,000 to 10,000 employees
- Job titles or functions and seniority: Finance, IT, Data; Director and above
- Exclusions: Current customers, competitors
- Expected audience size (after matching): about 38,000 members

## 4. Creative and copy
| Ad | Format | Intro text | Headline | Visual | CTA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | Single image | Bad data costs mid-size companies more than they think. See your number in 2 minutes. | What does bad data cost you? | Calculator screenshot with a dollar figure | Download |
| B | Single image | Bad data costs mid-size companies more than they think. See your number in 2 minutes. | Find the cost of bad data in 2 minutes | Calculator screenshot with a dollar figure | Download |

- Message and proof points used: "Teams spend 30% of their week fixing data" (2025 customer survey, n=120)
- Which past learning this applies: question headlines beat statement headlines on CTR in Q4 2025 (see creative performance log)

## 5. Test plan
- Variable being tested: headline (question vs. statement)
- Versions: A (question), B (statement); everything else identical
- How we'll read it: CTR in week 3 as direction; leads per 1,000 clicks at week 8 decides
