# Landing page template (gated offer)

> Template. The field names in brackets are the `field` values used in the copy check, so a draft can be checked with `python3 -m assistant copy`.

1. **Page title for search results** [meta_title]: 60 characters or fewer.
2. **Meta description** [meta_description]: 155 characters or fewer.
3. **Headline** [headline]: the outcome or the question the offer answers. 60 characters or fewer.
4. **Subheadline** [subheadline]: what it is, how long it takes, what you get. 160 characters or fewer.
5. **What you get** [bullet]: 3 to 5 bullets, 120 characters or fewer each. Concrete outputs, not features.
6. **How it works** [step]: 3 short steps.
7. **Proof** [proof]: approved proof points only, up to 2.
8. **Form:** as few fields as sales operations allows. Say what happens after submit.
9. **Button** [cta]: verb first, 30 characters or fewer. Same words as the ads' CTA.
10. **After submit:** the thank-you page and the next step.

Rules: one offer and one CTA per page. No navigation menu on paid traffic pages. Keep the hero and form visible on a phone screen without scrolling past the headline.
