# Master brief: 2026-Q3 Data Readiness

> Made-up example. Fernhill Software is a fictional company. This brief has a few gaps and conflicts on purpose, so the assistant has something to flag.

## Campaign

- **Campaign name:** 2026-Q3 Data Readiness
- **Product:** Fernhill Data Quality Platform
- **Launch date:** 2026-07-01
- **End date:** 2026-09-30
- **Owner:** Integrated Marketing
- **Total budget:** $120,000
- **Primary CTA:** Take the 3-minute Data Readiness Assessment

## Objective

Generate qualified pipeline from mid-size retail and financial services companies whose data isn't ready for AI projects. Get target-account buyers to take the Data Readiness Assessment, then move high scorers to a demo.

## KPIs

| KPI | Target |
|---|---|
| Assessment completions | 600 |
| MQLs | 180 |
| Sales accepted leads (SALs) | 60 |
| Pipeline | |

## Audience

- **Industries:** Retail, Financial Services
- **Company size:** 1,000 to 10,000 employees
- **Regions:** United States and Canada
- **Personas:** VP of Data, Director of Analytics, CMO
- **Target accounts:** 400 accounts on the Q3 target account list
- **Exclusions:** Current customers, competitors, companies under 1,000 employees

## Pain points

- AI projects stall because customer data is duplicated, incomplete or spread across systems.
- Teams spend more time fixing data than using it.
- Leaders can't tell whether their data is ready for AI until a project fails.

## Message

- **Primary message:** Know if your data is ready for AI before you start.
- **Reasons to believe:**
  - Assessment benchmarks you against 500+ mid-size companies [CONFIRM source]
  - Customers cut data cleanup time by 40% (2025 customer survey, n=120)
  - Setup in under two weeks

## Creative concept and tone

- **Concept:** "Ready or not": a simple readiness score, shown as a gauge.
- **Tone:** Plain, confident, practical. No hype.
