# Master brief: 2026-Q3 Clean Customer Data

> Made-up example. Fernhill Software is a fictional company. This brief has a few gaps and conflicts on purpose, so the assistant has something to flag.

## Campaign

- **Campaign name:** 2026-Q3 Clean Customer Data
- **Product:** Fernhill Data Quality Platform
- **Launch date:** 2026-07-01
- **End date:** 2026-09-30
- **Owner:** Integrated Marketing
- **Total budget:** $120,000
- **Primary CTA:** Download the free Customer Data Cleanup Playbook

## Objective

Generate qualified pipeline from mid-size retail and financial services companies with duplicate and incomplete customer data. Get target-account buyers to download the Customer Data Cleanup Playbook, then move engaged accounts to a demo.

## KPIs

| KPI | Target |
|---|---|
| Playbook downloads | 600 |
| MQLs | 180 |
| Sales accepted leads (SALs) | 60 |
| Pipeline | |

## Audience

- **Industries:** Retail, Financial Services
- **Company size:** 1,000 to 10,000 employees
- **Regions:** United States and Canada
- **Personas:** VP of Data, Director of Analytics, CMO
- **Target accounts:** 400 accounts on the Q3 target account list
- **Exclusions:** Current customers, competitors, companies under 1,000 employees

## Pain points

- The same customer shows up as several records across systems.
- Teams spend more time fixing data than using it.
- Reports don't match, so leaders stop trusting the numbers.

## Message

- **Primary message:** One customer, one record. Clean up duplicate customer data for good.
- **Reasons to believe:**
  - The playbook is based on 500+ customer cleanups [CONFIRM source]
  - Customers cut data cleanup time by 40% (2025 customer survey, n=120)
  - Setup in under two weeks

## Creative concept and tone

- **Concept:** "One customer, five records": the same person shown as five slightly different records.
- **Tone:** Plain, confident, practical. No hype.
